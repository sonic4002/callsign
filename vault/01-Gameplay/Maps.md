# Maps

There are four maps, all generated by `tools/mapgen.py` into the `maps` block of `shared/rules.json` and chosen in the lobby. Each is drawn on its west half and mirrored west to east, so neither side starts better.

## What every map has

- Headquarters at Alpha (west, side 0) and Zulu (east, side 1).
- A sea rig at Sierra, taken only by boats or aircraft, paying 30 gold a second ([[Economy]]).
- Points of three capturable kinds: `landRig` (taken by infantry), `point` (infantry or vehicles) and `seaRig` (naval or air). The names are historical; see [[Economy]].
- Each point has an id letter, a callsign in both languages, a local name in both languages and spoken aliases ([[Map Point Letters]]).
- Landmarks (houses, bunkers, ruins, groves, rocks, walls) with generated names such as "the ruin north of Tango" / "החורבה מצפון לתמר" ([[Terrain, Cover and Bridges]], [[Places on the Radio]]).

## Routes across

From the generator and commit `532aef2`:

- **Twin Bridges**: a frozen ford in the north (Golf), two bridges through the town island in the middle (Foxtrot), a south bridge, a quarry point on each flank, a small gold field on each coast road.
- **Frozen Lake**: two rivers running from a frozen lake to the sea with open country between them; two northern and two central bridges.
- **Mountain Passes**: a glacier round the north end of the range (November), the middle pass (Golf), the open south gap (Victor), and a river with a lake across the south with four bridges.
- **Great Moat** (from the generator, added 2026-11-27): a river from edge to edge with a town on an island in its middle; through the island by its west and east bridges, down the north road past the mine (Golf) or over the frozen moat, behind the northern range over the glacier (November), and through the south country by the far bridges and the island's south bridges.

## What the generator guarantees

`tools/mapgen.py` checks, among other things (commit `532aef2`): the base layout is open land for the widest structure on both sides; the dockyard's shore is not a tie; the bases stay joined with every bridge down and with the ice gone; every spot is within an exact offset of a point (`SPOT_REACH` = 900); every piece of ground has something with a name within 560 (`NAME_REACH`).

- `make maps` rewrites the maps in `shared/rules.json`.
- `python3 tools/mapgen.py --show` draws them as text and changes nothing.

## Length and pace

A room uses the map's `lengthSeconds` when nobody chose a length; the lobby accepts 120 to 1800 seconds (`server/room/hub.go`), so the huge map's 30 minutes is the most a map can ask for. `pace` multiplies how fast everything moves so that a bigger map is not a long walk. A match's speed option (relaxed 0.75, normal 1, fast 1.5) is a different thing: it changes only how much real time a tick takes ([[Sim Loop and Determinism]]).

## History

The first maps were smaller, with default lengths of 10, 15 and 20 minutes and paces 1, 1.5, 1.9 (commit `effe887`). The three bigger battlefields replaced them on 2026-11-24 (commit `532aef2`), and the large map was reshaped after it went to Ashfall three times in four (commit `0902aab`): see [[Balance]].

A fourth map, Great Moat (`huge`), was added on 2026-11-27 with six more point letters; until then Mountain Passes was the largest map and fifteen letters were in use. See [[Map - Huge]] and [[Map Point Letters]].

## Differs from the plan

`docs/PLAN.html` has one map with nine points (A, B, G, D, H, F, K, S, Z) where Golf is a bridge, Hotel a forest and Kilo an airfield. The local names now differ per map, and twelve more letters are in use (L, M, N, T, V, Y, and on the huge map C, E, O, Q, W, X).
